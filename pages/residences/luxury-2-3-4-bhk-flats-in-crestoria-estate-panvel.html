L&T Realty Crestoria Estate - Panvel

Estate Living, Now in Panvel

L&T Realty Crestoria Estate redefines estate-living in Panvel with a refined collection of 2, 3, and 4-bedroom residences. This luxurious project serves as a gateway to an oasis of serenity, featuring Spanish-inspired architecture marked by majestic arches and inviting courtyards. The landscape transitions seamlessly from the structured elegance of the Grand Promenade to the fluid, organic spaces of the Wildwood Sanctuary, balancing palatial grace with nature’s embrace. Beyond the residential zones, the estate offers unparalleled convenience with a curated selection of premium fashion labels, fine-dining, and chic cafés. Positioned near the Navi Mumbai International Airport and the upcoming metro network, Crestoria Estate provides a sophisticated, connected environment that remains a timeless sanctuary of calm.
