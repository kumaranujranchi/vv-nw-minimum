L&T Realty The Gateway - Parel Ext. : Luxurious 3, 4 and 4.5 BHK Flats

Experience Panoramic Sea-View in Parel Ext.

Standing tall amidst the grandiose skyline of South Mumbai, The Gateway, is the epitome of sophistication and opulence. Enter the gateway of luxury with 3, 4 and 4.5 BHK apartments in Parel ext. that face the ocean, offering uninterrupted views of the Arabian Sea as well as the world-renowned mudflats. Elevate your lifestyle with a large variety of indoor and outdoor amenities and live life to the fullest with the spacious clubhouse. A rare opportunity awaits those who will soon call these distinguished homes their own.

  • 2 iconic towers of 60+ storeys
  • 40,000 sq. ft. Sky Clubhouse with premium amenities
  • Masterfully crafted grand 3, 4 & 4.5 bed residences
