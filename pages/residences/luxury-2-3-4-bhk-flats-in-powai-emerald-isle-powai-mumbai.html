Elevated Living at Veridian, Emerald Isle – Powai’s Thriving Address

Where Luxury Meets Community

Emerald Isle in Powai is not just a project, but a flourishing residential address. It is now a completed landmark community alive with 1500+ happy families. Spread across 19 acres with 10 acres of open spaces and 3 acres of central greenery, it offers an environment designed for both elegance and wellbeing. The residents here enjoy the best of connectivity and convenience with schools, hospitals, metro stations, and commercial hubs close at hand. What began as a vision of premium living has today evolved into a vibrant lifestyle destination.
