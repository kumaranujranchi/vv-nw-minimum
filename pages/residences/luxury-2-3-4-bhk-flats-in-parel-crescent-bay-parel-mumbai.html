South Mumbai Life in the heart of Parel

Crescent Bay, Parel is one of L&T Realty’s most premium projects offering luxury flats in South Mumbai. This marquee project consists of 2, 3 & 4 BHK luxury flats in Parel, Mumbai which enables you to lead the life of your dreams. The ultra-luxurious gated community consists of six towers designed to form a crescent, offering a lifetime of uninterrupted east and west panoramic views. The towers in Crescent Bay are equipped with a plethora of amenities from swimming pools to a unique Skydeck that consists of a jogging track where you can enjoy your morning cardio 300ft. above sea level. Crescent Bay by L&T Realty also offers seamless connectivity to major highways and the upcoming Sewri-Nhava Sheva Trans Harbour Link to make your commutes around the city a breeze....

The premium gated community comprising luxurious 2, 3, and 4 BHK flats in Mumbai houses some of the city’s most influential residents. Moreover, the 2 bed ready-to-move-in homes are Parel’s largest#, thus redefining urban sophistication.

L&T Realty Crescent Bay offers ready-to-move-in premium 3 BHK in Parel as well as 4 BHK luxury flats in the Signature Tower (T6) and 2 & 3 BHK flats in Mumbai in the premium tower (T3).

The Signature Tower now takes fine living to new heights with the launch of Palatial Suites. Each 4 BHK in Parel provides extra spacious luxury that is an ode to your stature. These apartments in Mumbai are replete with luxuries like a spacious sun deck, walk-in wardrobe and thoughtfully designed interiors. These homes are also equipped with an aesthetically designed modular kitchen which is befitting to your refined taste in luxury. Equipped with the highest quality fittings that complement the sprawling views and lavish amenities, the exclusive residences are set to be the most desirable address in South Mumbai.
