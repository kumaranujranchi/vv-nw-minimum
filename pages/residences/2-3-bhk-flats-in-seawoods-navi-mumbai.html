Built on the concept of futuristic living, L&T Seawoods Residences, is a ready-to-move-in residential project in Navi Mumbai. Designed for fine living, the 2 & 3 BHK flats for sale in Seawoods offer a mélange of comfort and style within the most modern gated community in Navi Mumbai. Developed on India’s first Transit Oriented Development, L&T Seawoods Residences is located alongside Seawoods Grand Central Mall, offices, Seawoods Station and is just a couple of kms away from Palm Beach Road. This property is also well connected to social infrastructure through wide roads. Embodying the true meaning of convenience, living at the Seawoods Residences is all that’s truly needed for new age living.

FAQs

L&T Realty’s Seawoods amenities include a range of features that enhance the living experience for residents of 2 & 3 BHK flats in Seawoods, Navi Mumbai. These include a swimming pool, gym, landscaped gardens, children’s play area, clubhouse, and more.

L&T Realty’s Seawoods speciality includes prime locations in a Transit Oriented Development (TOD) that make it easy to get to Palm Beach Road, Seawoods Grand Central Mall, offices and the railway station.

L&T Realty’s Seawoods security guarantees a gated community with many security measures.

It is possible to arrange a L&T Realty’s Seawoods site visit by contacting the sales office or by visiting their website.

L&T Realty’s Seawoods process of buying includes going to the site, choosing a unit, checking it out, completing the booking formalities and, if necessary, getting financing.
