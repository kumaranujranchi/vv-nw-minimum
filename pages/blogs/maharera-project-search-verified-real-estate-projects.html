MahaRERA Project Search: How to Find Verified Real Estate Projects Online

July 10, 2025
No Tags Assigned to this Post

Table Of Contents

When you are buying a home, due diligence is important. This means that you should verify that the property is legal and follows all the rules set by RERA: the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act. For properties in Maharashtra, you can do this by doing a simple MahaRERA project search. With the rise of real estate scams and delays in project delivery, this verification process will protect you from fraud, delays, and unapproved constructions.

By verifying the property using MahaRERA’s search project feature, you can confirm that the builder has the necessary approvals and financial disclosures, offers legal protection as required by RERA, and reduces the risk of investing in projects with poor records or legal issues.

MahaRERA is a regulatory body set up under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development Act), 2016, ensuring that the developers comply with rules regarding approvals, timelines, and disclosures. Real estate project verification in Maharashtra
will also give homebuyers access to real-time, authentic project information.

In this blog, we are going to learn more about how to find verified projects on MahaRERA: what it is, why it is important, and a step-by-step guide to finding your desired property on the website!

What is MahaRERA and What Makes it Crucial for Homebuyers?

MahaRERA stands for ‘Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority’, launched in May 2017 under the RERA Act.

Why Was It Established?

  • Protect buyers from misleading advertisements and false promises.
  • Make real estate more transparent.
  • Create accountability for builders and agents
  • Offer a platform to resolve disputes quickly and fairly.

Types of Projects Under MahaRERA

Here is a list of MahaRERA-registered projects:

  • Residential Projects (apartments, villas, and townships)
  • Plotted developments
  • Ongoing and under-construction projects, especially those that haven’t received Occupancy Certificates (OC)

No real estate project above 500 sq. meters or with more than 8 units can be marketed or sold unless registered with MahaRERA.

Why Should You Check MahaRERA Project Details Before Buying a Property?

Legal Protection and Transparency

  • Only RERA-registered projects are bound by the strict regulations
  • Developers must stick to declared timelines and commitments.
  • Any delays can lead to buyer compensation or penalties for the builder.

Fraud Prevention

  • Many builders announce pre-launch sales without approvals. MahaRERA eliminates such fraud by allowing you to verify that all permissions are in place. You can do this by checking the MahaRERA registration number of the project on the site.

Access to Critical Documents

When you verify property on the MahaRERA website, you get access to the following information:

  • Layout and building plans
  • Timeline for construction and completion
  • Legal title reports
  • Details of ongoing litigations
  • Booking and sales status

This information will allow you to make an informed decision and reduce the possibility of hidden costs or disputes.

Step-by-Step Guide to MahaRERA Project Search Without Login

An advantage here is that you don’t need an account on the website. You can do a MahaRERA search project without a login ID! Here is a step-by-step guide to searching for a MahaRERA project online:

Step 1: Visit the MahaRERA site
This is https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/


Step 2: Go to the ‘Registered Projects’ tab

Step 3: Select ‘Search Project Details’

Here are some filters you can use while doing a project search on MahaRERA:

  • Project Name
  • Promoter or Developer Name
  • RERA Registration Number
  • District, Tehsil, or Village
  • Project Type (Residential, commercial, or mixed)
  • Project status (Ongoing, completed, or lapsed)

You will easily get access to all project disclosures, including downloadable PDFs of submitted documents, timelines, and approvals.

How to Search MahaRERA Project Details by Registration Number or Promoter Name

Using RERA Registration Number

  • You will find this number on project brochures, hoardings, websites, or promotional material. For example, L&T Realty 77 Crossroads – Ghatkopar, has a MahaRERA number for each of its towers.
  • Paste this number directly into the MahaRERA project search bar.

Using Promoter or Builder Name

You can also use MahaRERA to search project details by using the developer’s name:

  • This shows that all projects registered by the builder, both active and completed.
  • It is also useful to check:

– Past delivery timelines
– Ongoing litigations
– Financial compliance
– Project delays or lapsed registration

Why it Matters: A builder’s credibility often reflects across all their projects. If a developer has several delayed or lapsed projects, it’s a red flag.

Understanding MahaRERA Project Information: What Each Section Means

When you open the MahaRERA project details, you see multiple tabs. Here is how to understand them:

MahaRERA project status:

  • Ongoing: Still under construction
  • Completed: Received the completion certificate or OC
  • Lapsed: Registered has expired, and the construction may be illegal or stalled

Important Dates:

  • Date of Commencement: When the construction began
  • Expected Completion Date: As promised by the builder
  • Actual Date of Completion: If available

Sanctioned Approvals

  • Building Permission
  • Environmental Clearance (if applicable)
  • Commencement Certificate
  • Land ownership proof or the title report

Financial Disclosures

  • Estimated project cost
  • Amount received from allottees
  • Amount withdrawn and percentage of work completed

Litigation Details

  • Legal disputes related to the land, development rights, or compliance
  • Helps assess the potential risk of the project being stuck.

Booking and Sales Status

  • Number of units sold v/s available
  • Shows if the project is fully sold out or still has unsold units

How to Verify Builder Registration with MahaRERA

Here are the steps you can check the builder registration on MahaRERA,

  • Go to MahaRERA Website —> Go to ‘Registered Projects’ —> Do a promoter-wise Search.
  • Enter the name of the MahaRERA-registered developer
  • Click on the individual projects listed under them.

Red Flags to Look Out For:

  • Projects marked as ‘lapsed’
  • Builders with no completed projects or a history of delays
  • Litigation-heavy profiles
  • Multiple projects that are missing financial updates

Each builder also has ‘Promoter Details,’ a page that shows their PAN, contact info, and project portfolio.

Searching MahaRERA Projects on Mobile Devices or Apps

Responsive Mobile Layout Overview
You can also do the MahaRERA website project search on your phone; the website is mobile-friendly and allows you to search for registered projects and agents as well as file complaints directly from your phone. The layout also adjusts well to small screens, making basic search, filtering, and project detail viewing on the go easy.

Bookmarking and Exporting Project Details
Another advantage of MahaRERA’s mobile-friendly project search is that you can also take screenshots and share project URLs. Some browsers may also allow users to save pages for offline viewing. Though official document downloads can only be done on the desktop version, bookmarking helps you keep track of verified projects while doing your search.

Checklist for Verifying a MahaRERA Registered Project

Now that you know how to verify property on the MahaRERA website, here is a checklist you can follow:

  • RERA ID Match: Confirm that the project’s advertised RERA registration number matches the one that has been listed on the MahaRERA website.
  • Approved Plans Uploaded: Review floor plans, layout plans, and building permissions, uploaded by the developer to ensure regulatory approvals
  • Completion Dates vs Actual Progress: Compare the committed completion date when you find the MahaRERA-registered project with the on-ground construction status or recent project photos.
  • No Pending Litigations: Check if the project has any legal cases or regulatory disputes, as this can affect delivery timelines and legal ownership.
  • Phase-wise Registration: If a large project is being launched in phases, then ensure that each phase has a separate MahaRERA ID. This will help keep accurate tracks and protect your rights.

Common Pitfalls in MahaRERA Project Verification and How to Avoid Them

On the flip side, there are some drawbacks to MahaRERA’s search project feature, such as:

  • Marketing Names v/s Registered Names: Developers often advertise catchy names, but the registered name under MahaRERA may be different.
  • Phases Not Registered Separately: Some builders may launch a second or third phase without registering them separately. Always verify that each tower or phase has its own valid registration.
  • Expired Registration Numbers Reused: Watch out for expired or lapsed MahaRERA numbers being used again in advertisements. Verify that the registration status is valid when you are doing a project search on MahaRERA by checking its last updated date.

How to Cross-Verify Projects from Real Estate Portals with MahaRERA

  • Matching the MahaRERA ID from Online Listings: Always locate the MahaRERA ID mentioned in property listings and cross-check it on the official portal to ensure its authenticity.
  • Confirming Real Developers and Project Location: Verify that the developer’s name and the project’s exact address match with the ones given in the MahaRERA listing. Mismatches are a red flag.
  • Validating Images and Floor Plans: Use uploaded documents on MahaRERA to cross-reference the floor plans, elevation designs, and master plans. This helps identify false or outdated representations.

MahaRERA vs Other State Platforms: A Quick Comparison

  • Features Unique to MahaRERA: MahaRERA is considered to be a transparent and user-friendly RERA portal. With detailed dashboards, downloadable documents, and complaint resolution mechanisms, it makes real estate project verification in Maharashtra easy.

Comparing MahaRERA to UP, Gujarat, and Karnataka RERA

MahaRERA UP RERA Gujarat RERA Karnataka RERA
Clean UI, easy filtering, and project-wise document uploads More focused on compliant tracking, and the search experience may vary User-friendly, but has slow updates Has registration details, but lacks real-time updates and document depth sometimes.

Best Practices for Pan-India Homebuyers: Remember to check the respective state’s official RERA site. Avoid relying solely on third-party platforms. Bookmark the official links and check the latest updated dates. You should also compare the information across multiple sources.

Conclusion: Why MahaRERA Project Search is a Must for Every Homebuyer

Verifying a property on the MahaRERA website is more than just a legal formality; it is your first step toward making an informed decision. When you check the registration details, approved plans, and timelines, you protect yourself from delayed possession, legal disputes, and fraudulent schemes.

Always verify the MahaRERA registration number, and remember to review the uploaded legal documents and timelines. Do not fall for marketing traps; only trust official listings.

Checking and verifying all the information only takes extra minutes, but it will save you from a lot of legal and financial stress. Make the MahaRERA verification a non-negotiable part of your home buying process!

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I search for a project on the MahaRERA website?

  • You can use the official portal: https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/ and search by project name, promoter name, or registration number.

2. Can I check MahaRERA project details without logging in?

  • Yes, project details and documents are publicly accessible without a login.

3. Where do I find the MahaRERA registration number for a project?

  • It is usually mentioned in advertisements or brochures. If not, you can ask the builder directly or check on the MahaRERA site using the project name.

4. How can I verify a builder on MahaRERA?

  • Use the ‘Search by Promoter’ option to view all registered projects, ongoing works, and any compliance issues linked to the builder.

5. What documents are visible on a MahaRERA project listing?

  • You can access legal approvals, layout and floor plans, title reports, carpet area statements, draft agreements, and project updates.

6.What does “lapsed” project mean on MahaRERA?

  • A lapsed project means that the registration on a project has expired, and the developer hasn’t renewed it. This is an indicator of delays or non-compliance.

7. Can I download documents from the MahaRERA website?

  • Yes. You can download key documents like layout plans and permissions in PDF format from the project details page.

8. Are all builders required to register under MahaRERA?

  • Yes, for residential and commercial projects over a specific area or number of units, builders have to register under MahaRERA. Non-compliance will be a violation under the RERA Act.

9. How often is the MahaRERA project data updated?

  • Developers have to submit quarterly progress updates, but actual update frequency may be different. Check the ‘last updated’ date on each project listing.

10. Is MahaRERA applicable to commercial real estate?

  • Yes. MahaRERA rules apply to both residential and commercial real estate projects, including shops, offices, and mixed-use developments.
