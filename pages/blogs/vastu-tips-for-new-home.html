Vastu Tips for New Home: Secrets to Perfect Harmony Inside

December 20, 2024
No Tags Assigned to this Post

Table Of Contents

New homes mark new beginnings and one of the many ways new homeowners bring harmony into their homes is through Vastu. Vastu Shastra is the ancient Indian science of architecture and design, used to harmonise living spaces with natural energies. It originated around 5000 years ago and combines astronomy, geometry and energy flow to help create a balanced environment, aligning structures with cosmic forces to ensure health and happiness.

In this blog, we will explore some Vastu tips for a new home, but first, let’s explore the importance of this ancient science in modern homes.

Vastu Shastra is gaining more attention in modern home designs; homeowners are turning to it as it gives practical solutions for enhancing the well-being of residents.

By promoting stability in a living space, it supports mental peace, productivity and overall health. Adhering to Vastu principles can create spaces that nurture positivity and harmony.

Comprehensive Vastu Tips for a New Home

Here are some basic Vastu tips for a home for you to follow:

  • Structural Design and Layout
  • The main building should align with the main directions for optimal energy alignment.
  • Open spaces should also ideally be in the north and east, while heavy structures can be built in the south or west.
  • Room-Specific guidelines:
  • Living Room:
  • The living room should face the north, east or northeast direction.
  • Place your sofa to face east or north, as these directions promote positivity. Don’t clutter and keep the space well-lit.
  • Kitchen:
  • Ideally, the kitchen should be in the southeast corner, as the direction represents fire.  
  • Use warm colours like orange or red for the kitchen decor. The cooking stove should face east.
  • Bedroom:
  • Make sure that the master bedroom is in the southwest, for stability. Avoid bedrooms in the northeast.
  • Sleep with your head towards the south or east for better health. Use soft and calming colours like light blue or green.
  • Bathrooms and Toilets
  • Construct bathrooms in the west or northwest to minimise any negative energy.  
  • Ensure proper ventilation and use light shades for walls to maintain freshness.
  • Staircases and Elevators
  • The staircases should ascend clockwise and face the south or west. This will help avoid energy disruption.
  • Don’t place the staircase in the northeast.
  • Basement and Storage Areas
  • Keep the basement in the north or east directions, as they help prevent negative energy.
  • Use the space for storage, but avoid living in the area.

Vastu Tips for Home Entrance

Your new home’s main door represents your first steps into the next phase of your life. Here are some Vastu tips for home entrance, that you can follow:

  • Main Door Design and Material:
  • Use robust materials like wood for the main door, and always keep it polished. Avoid using metal doors for the entrance.
  • The door should open clockwise and be free from any obstructions.
  • Entrance Décor:
  • Add auspicious symbols like the Om at the entrance for spiritual protection.
  • You can also place a plant, like the Tulsi, near the doorway for positivity, and ensure proper lighting in the area.
  • Do’s and Don’ts:
  • Avoid placing trash bins or shoes close to the main door of your house.
  • Ensure that the door doesn’t creak when you open or close it, and always check for any damages.

Enhancing Interiors with Vastu

  • Colour Psychology:
  • Paint the walls in the north and east directions with lighter colours. This includes white, cream or light green, to promote calmness.
  • Avoid any dark or depressing colours like black or deep grey.
  • Furniture Arrangement:
  • Keep all the heavy furniture in the south or west sections of the room. It will help balance energy.
  • Make sure that the furniture is not placed under any beams, to help avoid stress.
  • Decorative Elements:
  • Use mirrors on the north or east walls. Avoid placing them opposite your bed, as it is believed this can create a restless environment and thus disrupt sleep.
  • Place indoor plants like bamboo, money or succulents, as they will help boost positivity.

Vastu for Outdoor Spaces

The outdoor space surrounding your home is just as important as what’s inside. Keeping them Vastu optimised so that you are always surrounded by harmony and positivity.

  • Garden and Landscaping:
  • Place gardens in the north or east. This will give you spiritual and health benefits.
  • Plant fruit-bearing trees in the south. Avoid planting thorny plants such as the cactus.
  • Water Features:
  • Locate fountains or ponds in the northeast, as it will help attract wealth and prosperity.
  • The water in these features should flow in the north or east directions.
  • Garage and Parking:
  • Always position the garage of your home in the northwest, and align the vehicles to face the north or east when you park it.

Vastu Remedies for Existing Homes

Vastu Shastra is versatile; no rule states that vastu applies only to new houses or under-construction residential buildings. If you have been living in a flat for a few years and have just noticed mistakes, here are some basic vastu tips for your home.

  • Corrections without reconstruction: Many homes have existing Vastu defects that cannot be corrected through structural changes. However, there are some simple remedies you can follow:
  • Mirrors: Use mirrors strategically; they can deflect negative energy and increase positivity. You can place a mirror on the north wall and enhance the chances of financial prosperity.
  • Colours: Change the wall paint or décor colours to shades that increase harmony. So if your walls are painted black or grey, go for pastel shades, such as lavender or mint green.
  • Furniture Placement: Rearrange the furniture in a way that aligns with Vastu principles, and avoid cluttering the north and east directions.
  • Salt Therapy: You can also keep a bowl of sea salt in areas with the most negative energy. Remember to change the bowl every week to cleanse the space.  
  • Lighting: Brighten up the dark spaces, especially in the northeast, with natural or artificial light to energise the space.
  • Use of Vastu Tools:
  • Yantras: Install sacred geometric diagrams, such as the Shri Yantra, in your home. They help harmonise energy and attract prosperity.
  • Crystals: You can also place certain crystals, like quartz or amethyst in some areas, as they will absorb and transform all negative energy.
  • Wind Chimes: Hang wind chimes in the northwest or northeast to enhance energy flow.
  • Pyramids: Objects shaped as a pyramid will help in countering the Vastu defects, and are useful when placed in the centre of the home.

Integrating Vastu with Sustainable Design

Many modern homebuyers who seek to build or buy eco-friendly homes, also want to make vastu changes that align with sustainable practices. Here are some basic Vastu tips for a green home.

  • Eco-Friendly Materials
  • Use natural materials like bamboo, clay or stone. These materials align with the Vastu principles of natural harmony.
  • Avoid using synthetic or chemically treated materials, as they can block the flow of energy.
  • Energy Efficiency:
  • Incorporating solar panels in the east or west to harness natural energy without disrupting Vastu principles.
  • Designing windows and ventilation systems in the north and east to maximise natural light and airflow, thus reducing energy consumption.
  • Rainwater harvesting in the northeast section will help enhance the water energy and support sustainability.

Debunking Common Vastu Myths and Misconceptions

Although Vastu Shastra has been around for centuries, many still believe in myths and misconceptions that often lead to misinformation. Let’s prove some of them wrong:

Myth

Fact

Every Vastu defect leads to misfortune

Not true; there are remedies that can help fix these defects, and not all of them can lead to negative outcomes.

Vastu applies only to homes

Vastu principles are applicable to commercial spaces, gardens as well as vehicles.

Interestingly, there is also a modern scientific angle to Vastu Shastra:

  • Vastu principles align with modern physics; one example is that both emphasise the importance of directions for light and airflow.
  • It also states the significance of natural elements like water and air, much like ecological and psychological well-being principles.
  • Scientific studies have suggested that a clutter-free and naturally lit space can improve mental health, which aligns with Vastu Shastra’s core principles.

Conclusion

Vastu Shastra principles dictate the importance of interconnecting space and energy to our well-being. By incorporating them, your home can become a sanctuary for mental peace, physical health and spiritual growth.

Start small with any Vastu changes in your home, especially in case extensive changes are not possible. Start with simple remedies such as decluttering your space, or repainting the walls in different, brighter colours. In case you are unsure about how to apply Vastu principles, consult experts for advice that will suit your home.

Align Vastu with your preferences by integrating it with modern home-building techniques, such as sustainable and energy-efficient designs.

Your final aim is to create a space that supports happiness, success and harmony. By embracing Vastu principles, create a home that looks beautiful, and radiates positive energy, which will help you contribute to a fulfilling and balanced lifestyle.
