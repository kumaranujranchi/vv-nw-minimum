BBMP Property Tax Online Payment: A Step-by-Step Guide for Bengaluru Homeowners

July 23, 2025
No Tags Assigned to this Post

Table Of Contents

Paying the property tax is an essential part of owning real estate, and each city in India has its own municipal body that collects this tax from the residents. In Bengaluru, this body is called the BBMP, or the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike.

To define, the BBMP is a municipal corporation that is responsible for the civic administration and infrastructural development of Greater Bangalore. It governs core functions: sanitation, road maintenance, solid waste management, public health, and most importantly, property tax collection.

In this blog, we will learn about how you can do a BBMP property tax online payment, but first, let us explore the important role this corporation plays in urban governance:

  • Maintenance of roads, drainage, and footpaths.
  • Urban planning and approvals for building construction.
  • Management of parks, street lighting, and public amenities.
  • Property registration and documentation (Katha issuance)
  • Collection of revenue through property tax.

Paying the property tax is incredibly important as well:

  • It is a legal requirement under the Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act.
  • It also contributes to the city’s development directly, by aiding in road development, garbage collection, and sewage treatment.
  • Helps maintain ownership legality and helps in smooth resale, transfer, or loan procedures.

There are also penalties for non-compliance:

  • Interest at 2% per month on the defaulted amount.
  • Legal notices and property blacklisting.
  • Restrictions in availing civic services like Katha transfers.

Understanding BBMP Property Tax: An Overview for Homeowners

The BBMP Property Tax is an annual tax levied on property owners in Bengaluru to fund civic amenities. The tax is based on the unit area value system, which takes into account location, usage, and type of construction.

Who Pays This Tax?

  • Residential Property Owners (Flats, villas, independent houses). So when you buy a luxurious apartment at L&T Realty Elara Celestia – Bengaluru, you will have to pay the BBMP property tax for the same.
  • Commercial Establishments (Shops, offices, and warehouses)
  • Vacant Landowners
  • Mixed Properties, i.e., properties with both residential and commercial facilities.

Tax Assessment Period

  • It follows the financial year cycle; April 1st to March 31st.
  • Annual self-assessment is required, even if there is no change in property details.

Who Should Pay BBMP Property Tax, and When?

Who Needs to Pay BBMP Property Tax?

  • All property owners within the BBMP limits.
  • BBMP tax payment also includes new buyers (after registration) and those with under-construction properties.
  • Owners with Katha or PID numbers.

BBMP Property Tax Timeline

  • Due Date: April 30th of every financial year.
  • Rebate Period: 5% rebate if the tax is paid in full before April 30th.
  • Payments can also be made twice a year, although without a rebate.

Penalties for Delay

  • 2% interest has to be paid per month in case of outstanding dues.
  • Non-payment for multiple years can lead to legal notices.

Step-by-Step BBMP Property Tax Online Payment Guide

Let’s take a look at this detailed guide on how to pay BBMP Property Tax online:

Step 1

Visit the Official BBMP Portal at https://bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in/

Step 2

Enter your PID or SAS Application Number.

Step 3

View the property details and tax summary.

Step 4

Check for arrears or adjustments.

Step 5

Choose a BBMP property tax online payment method from the following:

  • Credit or Debit Card
  • Net Banking
  • UPI

Step 6

Download the payment receipt. An email confirmation is also sent, but always keep a soft and hard copy for records.

How to Pay BBMP Property Tax Offline

Alternatively, you can also do the BBMP tax payment offline.

Where to Pay Offline?

  • BBMP Help Centres
  • BangaloreOne/KarnatakaOne Centre
  • Authorised Bank Branches, like at Canara Bank or Corporation Bank.

Documents Required

  • Previous year tax receipt.
  • Property ID or Katha number
  • Owner ID proof (in case of new property)

Steps

  • Visit the nearest BBMP centre with the required documents.
  • Fill the SAS form manually, if not already submitted.
  • Make the payment via cash, cheque, or a Demand Draft.
  • Collect the printed receipt immediately.

How to Track BBMP Tax Payment Status and Get Receipts

How to Check Status

  • Go to the BBMP tax portal, and select ‘Payment Status’
  • Enter the application or transaction reference number.

Receive Confirmation

  • An email or SMS alert is sent after a successful transaction.
  • The receipt is also downloadable from the portal under ‘Print Receipt.’

Lost Receipt?

  • In case you lose the receipt, visit the portal and enter the PID or application ID.
  • Click on ‘Retrieve Old Receipts’ for the past years.

Understanding BBMP PID Number: How to Find and Use It

What is a PID Number?

  • The Property Identification Number (PID) is a 15-digit number assigned to every property under BBMP jurisdiction.

How to Find Your PID

  • Your BBMP property ID is available on the previous tax receipts.
  • Use the BBMP online portal’s ‘Search by Address or Owner Name.’
  • Check with the local ward office if the PID is not yet assigned.

Relation to Katha Number

  • The Katha is a document, while the BBMP property ID is a digital identifier.
  • PID is required to pay the BBMP property tax online.

BBMP SAS Property Tax System Explained

What is SAS?

  • The Self-Assessment Scheme was launched to allow property owners to declare and calculate their property tax annually.

Who Should Use SAS?

  • All property owners under BBMP limits, especially those with new properties, property alterations, or changes in use.

How SAS works

Here is a BBMP SAS property tax payment guide:

  • Choose the right SAS form (Form I, II, III)
  • Fill in the property dimensions, use type, built-up area, etc.
  • Submit it online or offline.
  • The system will auto-generate the tax payable.

BBMP Property Tax Forms and Their Use Cases

Form I

For properties already under the BBMP with a PID.

Form II

For properties that don’t have a PID but have a Katha number.

Form III

For new or unregistered properties without a PID or Katha

Use Cases

  • New owners can fill out Form III.
  • Form I for existing property with updated dimensions.
  • For a vacant site with Katha but no BBMP property ID, Form II will be ideal.

Where to Download Forms

  • Go to the BBMP Property Tax Portal and click on the ‘Downloads Section.’
  • Hard copies will be available at BBMP help centres.

BBMP Property Tax Slabs and Rates in Bengaluru (With Examples)

The BBMP tax payment process also requires you to understand the tax slabs and how the rates are calculated.

This municipal corporation uses the Unit Area Value method. So:

Annual Tax = Unit Area Value x the Built-up Area x 10 months.

Zonal Classification (As per location)

  • Zone A: Premium areas such as MG Road or Indiranagar.
  • Zone B: Mid-Level locations like Banashankari or HSR Layout.
  • Zone C: Developing zones, like Kengeri or Yelahanka.
  • Zone D: Moderately developed zones, like Mahadevpura or RR Nagar
  • Zone E: Emerging, semi-developed areas, like Hebbal.

An Example Calculation

Say, you are buying a 1000 sq. ft home in Zone E, in an area like Hebbal.

The approximate tax will be:

1.20 x 1000 x 10 = Rs 12,000.

Tax Rebates and Exemptions You Can Avail in Bangalore

Early Payment Rebate

You can avail a 5% BBMP tax rebate by paying the entire annual tax before April 30 of the financial year. This applies to both online and offline payment.

Exemptions Available

There are also BBMP property tax exemptions for certain categories of citizens and property types.

Beneficiary Exemption Details
Senior Citizens Partial exemption on self-occupied residential properties for owners aged 60+
Freedom Fighters Full exemptions on residential properties
Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribes (Below Poverty Line) Partial to full exemption, subject to verification.
Persons with Disabilities Up to 100% exemption, depending on disability type and extent.
Educational Institutions/Religious Properties/Charitable Trusts Full exemptions if no income is derived from the property

Documents Required

  • Age proof for senior citizens
  • Disability certificate for PwDs.
  • ID and income proof (for SC/ST category)
  • Legal documents (ownership proof, Katha, etc.)
  • Supporting letter or certification (for NGOs/Trusts/Religious groups)

How to Correct Errors in BBMP Tax Records

Common Issues

  • Name or address mismatch.
  • Incorrect PID or Katha number mapping
  • Wrong built-up area
  • Errors in usage type (residential vs commercial)
  • Multiple BBMP property IDs for the same record.

How to Correct BBMP Tax Details

  • Log in to https://bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in/
  • Click on ‘Raise Request’ or ‘Correction Request.’
  • Submit your property details along with supporting documents.
  • Alternatively, you can visit the BBMP Help Centre or Ward Office in Person.

Required Supporting Documents

  • To file a BBMP tax complaint, you will need the previous years’ paid tax receipts.
  • Katha certificate and extract.
  • Sale deed or property registration papers
  • Utility bill copy (for address verification)
  • Aadhaar card or owner ID proof.

BBMP Property Tax Helpdesk and Contact Information

BBMP Contact Methods

  • (080) 2297 5555 or (080) 2266 0000
  • Email: dcrev@bbmp.gov.in

In Person Support

  • You can visit the BBMP revenue offices located in your zone.
  • BangaloreOne and KarnatakaOne centres for basic services.
  • Ward Level Citizen Service Centres for document submissions.

Useful Links

  • BBMP Property Tax Portal: https://bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in/
  • To raise a grievance or service request: https://bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in/forms/grievancesreq.aspx

Keep in mind the following practices:

  • Always download and save your receipt.
  • Take a screenshot of the confirmation page
  • Cross-verify details with the previous year’s records.

Conclusion: Stay Compliant, Stay Empowered

When you pay the BBMP Property Tax, you help fund public infrastructure and services in your neighbourhood. Additionally, paying it will prevent any penalties and legal complications. It is also necessary for property resale, loans, Katha transfers, and to ensure legal ownership validity.

Digitally paying this tax is fast, convenient and safer than manual submission; you will be able to access the receipts and transaction history at any time. Moreover, it will also help contribute to the BBMP’s digital governance mission.

Remember: being tax compliant is not just a legal responsibility, but a civic duty!

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is BBMP Property Tax and who needs to pay it?
    • Anyone who owns a property, whether residential, commercial or a vacant land, within BBMP limits has to pay this annual tax.
  2. What documents are required for BBMP online tax payment?
    • You will need the previous year’s receipt, PID or Katha number, and Aadhaar card (to show proof for exemptions)
  3. What are the different payment methods available on the BBMP portal?
    • You can pay via credit or debit card, UPI, Net banking and wallet payments.
  4. Can I pay BBMP property tax offline?
    • Yes. You can go to the BBMP help centres, BangaloreOne centres or approved bank branches to pay the BBMP property tax offline.
  5. How is BBMP property tax calculated for residential properties?
    • Tax = Unit Area Value x Built-up area x 10 months, adjusted for depreciation and cess.
  6. How do I pay my BBMP property tax online?
    • Go to https://bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in/, enter your BBMP property ID, verify the details and pay using your preferred method.
  7. How do I change my name on my BBMP tax receipt?
    • Submit a correction request online, or visit your ward office with an ID proof and property documents.
  8. What is form 5 in BBMP property tax?
    • Form 5 is used to declare property modifications like change in built-up area, the use (residential to commercial), or ownership.
  9. Can I pay BBMP property tax without a PID?
    • Yes. Use your SAS application number or Katha number. It is recommended you have a PID to make future tracking easy.
  10. How do I find my BBMP zone for tax calculation?
    • Use the official BBMP ward list or the zonal classification tool on their portal.
  11. What if my BBMP payment fails but money is deducted?
    • Wait for 24 to 48 hours. If it is not resolved, you can contact them with the transaction ID and proof and get it resolved.
  12. How to apply for a property tax exemption under BBMP?
    • You can either visit your ward office, or submit a request on the BBMP portal required documents based on eligibility.
  13. Can I pay BBMP property tax in installments?
    • Yes. You can pay it in two half-yearly installments: April to September and October to March. However, rebates apply on full advance payment.
