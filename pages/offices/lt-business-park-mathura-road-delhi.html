L&T Business Park

Delhi Mathura Road

L&T Business Park is one of the premium (IT/ ITES) properties for lease at Mathura Road, New DelhiIt is a fine combination of convenience and proximity to strategic locations – being on the Delhi Mathura National Highway; it’s a minute walk to Sarai metro station and is in close proximity to South Delhi social infrastructure.This leasable office is designed for 24*7 business operations, is centrally air-conditioned and includes STPI, IT & ITES benefits for its occupants.

Features:

  • Grand Entrance with Double Height lobby
  • 8 common visitor meeting rooms in the lobby area
  • 8 High Speed Elevators with separate Service Elevator (Less waiting time for employees)
  • Separate entry and exit gates to the property with uniform traffic flow
  • Efficient electric supply management system with 100% DG backup and separate source of supply from DHBVN (electricity board)
  • 24/7 3 tier security, Designed for 24×7 operations
Key Differentiators
Only LEED Certified Gold rated green building in Faridabad
Well-connected to entire NCR
100 meters away from Sarai Metro station
Separate ingress & egress to the property with uniform traffic flow
